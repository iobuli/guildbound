Recorded sounds: Kenney "Impact Sounds" and "RPG Audio" (www.kenney.nl), CC0 / public domain.
The .ogg files in this folder are those recordings, used as they are by game/sfx.gd.

synth/*.wav are built by tools/make_sfx.py FROM those recordings (layered, varispeeded, filtered,
transient-shaped, reversed, granulated and given reverb tails), with a little synthesis as a
sweetener; the wind bed and the birds are synthesized (no recording covers them) though the wind's
leaf rustle is made of the cloth and paper recordings. Everything here is CC0 / public domain.
