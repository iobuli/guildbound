# Higher-timeframe patterns from the journal (`htf-patterns`)

Patterns the journal uses for direction and location that are not defined precisely enough to code: pin failure, hammer return, equal-swing targets, drift of doom.

Decides at **prior_bars**. Inputs: none. Outputs: none.

## htf-patterns.pin-failure

A pinbar that fails (the daily or weekly closes beyond the pin's head) is a direction signal the other way, used with an H1 LXPB at the head level.

## htf-patterns.undefined-terms

KPI, HoH, LoH and IWL are used without definition. Plausible readings: KPI = key price inflection, HoH / LoH = the head / low of a hammer or pin, IWL = inside-week low. None of these is confirmed.

## htf-patterns.eod-spike-day-after

After a spike candle, look for a short the next day; one of the best places to get in is the previous day's high. After a big down day a full reversal the next day is suspect for shorts.

## htf-patterns.key-levels-shallow-pullbacks

When key levels are near (many highs plus an all-time high), deep pullbacks are rare and shallow ones to the LXPB are more common.
