## equal-liquidity.pattern-for-tops

General pattern for spotting tops: a large run-up, an indecision candle (doji) at the peak, a good pullback, then price running back up but failing to make a new high.

*heuristic, observed, not coded.* Sources: webinar curated/notes-images.md (2024-10-19).
