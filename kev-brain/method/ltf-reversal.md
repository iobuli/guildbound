# M5 failed-breakdown reversal (Bund) (`ltf-reversal`)

A lower-timeframe reversal he trades a lot on the M5 Bund. It is not an SFP.

Decides at **prior_bars**. Inputs: none. Outputs: none.

## ltf-reversal.pattern

Price closes through the lows three times but immediately goes bid each time. "If the market is making fresh lows it should be a weak market, but it is immediately going bid." Weakness keeps getting bought.

## ltf-reversal.entry

Entry is a market buy at the first reversal candle after the third breakdown, with the stop just under the lows. No ATR or thrust candle is needed.

## ltf-reversal.m5-habits

M5 levels are marked and traded the same way as H1; M5 SFPs use a 12-period ATR; he does not hold M5 through news; the M5 stop trail can differ (a head-and-shoulders shoulder instead of the thrust candle).
