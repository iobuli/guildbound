not just any sweep will do, only sweeps of 'significant / deep' highs and lows count. but how do we define a 'deep swing'?

well for that, we use the current D1 ATR value, and the concept of 'swing in' and 'swing out'.

first of all, select whichever swing high youre currently looking that, that is being swept. and draw a line in either direction.
one all the way to the left (until you hit a candle) - this is the green line on the chart.and one all the way to the right (until you hit your sweep) - this is the orange line on the chart. [image]

you are going to draw the lowest point of each section: [image]

as you can see, each green and orange section has its own 'lowest point'.

these are called the 'swing in' and 'swing out' movements.

swing in // swing out: [image]

to see if a swing point is considered 'deep' or not. choose whichever side of the swing is smallest (in this case its the 'swing out') and measure it.

if it is equal to, or larger than, today's current ATR value, then it is considered a deep swing.

[image]

as you can see, this swing point comes out at 57.5, which is smaller than the day's ATR (66.8).

therefore this swing point is NOT considered deep enough to hinge a short trade off, if we sweep the high.
