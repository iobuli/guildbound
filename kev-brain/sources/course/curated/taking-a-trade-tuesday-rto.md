# tuesday-rto

**10/01/2025 12:50** RULES:tuesday onlyweekly open must not have been touched since 22:00 on monday. (no touching it so far on tuesday).buy / sell with a target of the weekly open.
GETTING IN A TRADE:buy / sell a multiple tap level.buy / sell an hourly pinbar.(weekly open above price = buy a bullish pin)
(weekly open below price = sell a bearish pin)
SETTING THE ORDERS:buy the top of the pin / sell the low of the pin.put your SL on the other side of the pinbar.only take trades that are minimum 1R (or more).
