# 3-touch-level

**21/10/2024 15:33** every support / resistance trade needs 3 confluences.

on a triple tap level, all 3 confluences are price touching your level: [image]

this is enough to take a trade

­

for our trading - we are focused on 'opposite last' levels.
which means that a level that was supporting price, once that level is broken, we expect it to act as the opposite - aka resistance [image]

a three touch level is formedthe level is broken throughprice retests the level from the other side

you short the level, typically at the last low created (see how the green line is perfectly centred on the last low - while the first and second lows are off by a few pixels).
