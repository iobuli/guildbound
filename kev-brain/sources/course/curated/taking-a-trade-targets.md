# targets

**22/10/2024 16:18** to choose a Target Price (TP) on a trade, we ask ourselves which is the first area on the chart that might cause some trouble

aptly named the First Trouble Area

yes, i could try to run every trade i take as far as possible [image]

but its much more consistent to take your profits at troublesome areas.

for my short here ^ i am looking to take profits at any support levels that may be in my way. in this case, im just looking at obvious levels that used to be resistance, but have now flipped over (into support)

and i see these two swing highs here that price had a strong reaction from: [image]

in general, price tends to gravitate to the most recent level. so even though price will run into the upper support level first - it is still a safe bet to target the lower (more recent) one. [image]

a lot of the time, however, its not quite so obvious.

take this 3 tap long for example: [image]

if we are looking for a place to TP on this setup, there is an extremely obvious level right above us [image]

however

that doesnt necessarily mean that it is the First Trouble Area

instead, this dotted level is clearly a pivotal level in the market - with price respecting it and bouncing off it time and time again [image]

its entirely likely that price will reach that dotted level - and reject entirely. leaving us wishing that we had just taken our easy money and not been greedy, trying to hold on for as much extra money as possible.

[image]

so the safest bet for an easy and steady income, is just to close the trade at this FTA ✅
