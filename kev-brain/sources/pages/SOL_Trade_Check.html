Findings

Things worth checking as you step through. The first two are the ones I'd want settled before this config gets money.

    Config. A level is a cluster of same-kind swing touches within 0.10× the prior day's ATR(20), needing 2+ counted touches (a touch counts only if it is 12+ bars from the last one and price travelled 0.50×ATR away in between). Price must CLOSE through the level by 0.10×ATR to break it, then travel 0.50×ATR away — the space rule — before a return counts. Entry is a limit at the level, filled only when price actually trades there. Stop goes under the bar that opened beyond the level and closed back through it, pushed past the level's own tolerance band. Target 4× risk. One position at a time.

    Fitting. space, target, touch count and stop style were searched on the first half only, scored net of 0.10R slippage, then run forward. The second half returned +$34,595/yr against the first half's +$46,188/yr.

    Causality. Levels are built and traded bar by bar; nothing reads a bar later than the decision bar. Every fill is at a price that traded. A bar containing both stop and target books as a loss.
