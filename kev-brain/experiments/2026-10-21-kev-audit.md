## C. Fills, costs, placebos (2022 harness run)

- Fills: 1323. Filled at a better price than the limit because the bar opened beyond it (gap): 0 (0%). That is how a resting limit fills; placebos use the same rule.
- Binance taker fees (0.1% a side) in R at a 0.5-ATR stop: median 0.040R, 90th percentile 0.059R. The flat 0.10R cost covers 100% of fills.
- Placebos: 2236 for 1323 fills; every placebo has a real counterpart on the same side: true.

## Verdicts
- **A, confirmed mistake:** the ATR-normalised trend called 65 of 69 days of the May-July 2021 crash "range" (it saw no down days at all). Fix: a second, percentage-based trend (`trendPct`: 50-day return and distance from the 90-day high or low). It labels that crash down on 24 days and 2022 down on 227 of 364 days. The old measure is kept and reported beside it. The new one is the trend the paper book uses.
- **B, no mistake:** the "levels are short-lived" finding does not depend on how levels are merged. Median life is 1.6-2.0 days and 48-49% are never tested, at merge tolerances of 0.1, 0.25 and 0.5 daily ATR.
- **C, no mistake:** there are no gap fills on 24/7 hourly crypto bars. The flat 0.10R cost exceeds real Binance taker fees on every fill (median 0.04R). Placebos match their real fills' sides.
- **Also fixed:** the atlas's unfinished last phase now ends at its extreme instead of the window edge.
