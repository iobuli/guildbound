# Nucleus trace: do the 18 Ichimoku lines tell where the hour closes? (pre-registered 2026-10-21, before any run)

## What is traced
The nucleus (`kev-lab/nucleus.py`, JS twin `kev-lab/web/nucleus-core.js`): 6 timeframes (1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, 1D) x 3 n-n-n Ichimoku lines (n = 9, 26, 52), read at every 1-minute close.
Features: `bal` (balance of the 18 lines, price above minus below), `slope_bal`, and the per-timeframe `bal` of each of the 6 timeframes.
States: 1h line order x 1h cloud position; all six timeframe balances the same sign (up / down) vs mixed.

## Outcome (data-derived scale, nothing chosen by hand)
- Hours: complete UTC hours only (all 60 minutes present); minute of hour mh = 0..58; only minutes where every line is valid.
- z_rem = ln(hour close / price at mh) / (sigma * sqrt(59 - mh)); sigma = std of 1-minute log returns over the prior 1440 minutes.
- Next hour: z_next = ln(next hour close / price at mh) / (sigma * sqrt(119 - mh)).

## Measures
- Per mh and feature: Spearman rho with z_rem, AUC of the feature for z_rem > 0, and a permutation band = 95th pct of |rho| over 100 shuffles of hours (one shared permutation matrix).
- Same for the next hour.
- States at mh in {0, 15, 30, 45, 55}: mean z_rem with day-cluster bootstrap CI and p (`patterns.boot`), BH q over all state rows (`patterns.bh`).

## Split
- Discovery: first valid minute .. 2021-12-31. Check: 2022. The check period is read once, same code.

## Rules
- Descriptive only, nothing tuned: no thresholds, weights or parameters are fitted.
- A curve "carries information" only if it rises above its permutation band in both periods with the same sign.
- 1m lines carry bid-ask bounce: a negative 1m rho is expected and is not tradable.

## Results (2026-10-21, `python3 kev-lab/trace.py`, 22 s, out/lab/trace.json)
- Tests: `tests.test_nucleus` 6/6 OK (brute force, no lookahead, forming vs closed, n-n-n collapse, 1m vs H1, JS/Python parity). No fix needed.
- Hours: discovery 10,319 complete hours, check 2022 8,760. Permutation band is about |rho| 0.019-0.022 at every minute.
- Rest of the hour: the effects are small and mostly negative, i.e. mean reversion. Price high in its lines leads to a weaker close.
  - 2022: every fast balance sits below its band most of the hour (mean rho -0.017 to -0.027 for bal, slope_bal, 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h; min about -0.06; AUC 0.475-0.48).
  - Discovery: flat on average (mean rho -0.004 to +0.011), crossing the band in both directions depending on mh.
  - Same sign above band in both periods: slope_bal 21/59 minutes, 1m.bal 11, 15m.bal 6, bal 5, 5m.bal 5, 1h.bal 1. 4h and 1D: never.
- Next hour: same picture, weaker in discovery. Same sign above band in both periods: slope_bal 16/59, 5m.bal 7, 1m.bal 3, 1h 2, 15m 2, bal 2. 2022 alone: slope_bal, 15m.bal, 1h.bal and bal are below their band at 32-39 minutes (mean rho about -0.02 to -0.03).
- States (1h order x cloud, six-TF alignment, at mh 0/15/30/45/55): none of 201 rows has BH q < 0.05.
- Reading: the nucleus does not point to where the hour closes. The only shared signal is a weak contrarian one (|rho| 0.02-0.05, AUC about 0.48), led by slope_bal and the fast timeframes. It is much stronger in 2022 than in 2020-21, so it is not stable.
  - Adjacent minutes are strongly dependent, so "minutes above band" overstates how many independent hits there are.
  - Part of the 1m effect is bid-ask bounce and not tradable.
  - Nothing here survives costs as a stand-alone rule.
