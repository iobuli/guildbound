Observations → representation

Each line keeps a window's high and low and compresses them to a midpoint and relative position. The closed midpoint, slope, historical shift and lag add temporal context. This loses the order and most of the path of the input prices. More derived values do not mean more independent information.

Temporal organization · preceding 30 calendar days

State = the joint ordering of the 9/26/52 midpoints on four timeframes. Six strict orders plus a tie per timeframe. This describes observed organization; it does not establish emergence or forecast skill.

State entropy measures variety. Conditional entropy measures remaining uncertainty in the next observed state given the previous state, using historical adjacent pairs only. Surprise measures the current state's rarity against earlier observations with add-one smoothing. These estimates have finite-sample bias and reflect shared inputs and overlapping windows.
