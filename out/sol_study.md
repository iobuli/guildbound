# SOL study: what the data says once every decision uses only earlier bars

Sources: SOLUSDT H1 (Binance, 2020-08 to 2026-10), the user's daily CSV, Trade Check page (43 sample trades), One Hour At A Time page (1,147 frames, 79 retest decisions), kev node graph and causal harness.

1. **Data trust.** One Hour candles match Binance H1 exactly (0 of 1,147 differ). Trade Check candles match until 2023-03, then another feed (up to 0.07 off). The daily CSV matches my daily bars by date; median difference 0.14%, p95 about 1%, volume about half of Binance: a different venue.
2. **Your engine vs kev.** One Hour counts a touch as a 3-bar swing point within 0.1 ATR and 12+ bars apart (recovered from its data; reproduces 40 of 83 counts exactly). Kev counts a touch only if price then travels 0.5 ATR away. Across 83 breaks: break bar agrees on 69, touch counts on 18 (kev fewer on 55). Details: `out/agree_spec.json`.
3. **Lookahead in both pages.** Trade Check keeps a trade only if its fill bar did not close through the level: 0 of 35 kept and 8 of 8 rejected match. One Hour's `far` equals the excursion over bars b+1..f including the decision bar on 79 of 79 decisions (54 of 79 from earlier bars only); 33 of 79 decision bars close through the level.
4. **Causal levels study** (`out/sol_levels.md`, 6,411 events, random-bar placebos): level fills average -0.09R, same as random orders. The fill-bar-closed-through split is -0.29R vs +0.07R, and random orders show -0.28R vs +0.07R: the filter manufactures the edge. Touch counts (either rule), age, side, regime, session, volume and year show no difference from random at q < 0.05. Space measured from earlier bars (0.5 ATR) beats random by about +0.09R in both halves but q = 1 and the net result is -0.02R.
5. **Spec replication** (`out/compat.md` equivalent in `out/compat.json`): SOL -0.23R (random orders -0.42R), gold -0.21R (random -0.31R). The harness ignores `space`, so only touches >= 2 and >= 3 differ.
6. **One Hour decisions, causal outcome** (79, exploratory): mean -0.34R (stop 0.5 ATR, 2R); T1 excursion rank-correlates +0.28 with outcome (permutation p 0.004); T2 to T4 do not. Treat as a lead only.
